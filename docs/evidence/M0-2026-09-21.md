# M0 verification — 2026-09-21

## Decision

**M0 remains blocked. Do not start M1 or treat this prototype as ready for daily use.** Reopening a freshly saved conversation failed three times, including on the final working version: Google showed the expected conversation text with different identifiers and a distinct history entry. The application refused to send a follow-up into an unconfirmed conversation. Matching text alone does not prove continuity.

The checks were performed by three GPT-5.6 Sol agents using high reasoning: one exclusive live-browser operator, one automated-test and fix author, and one independent reviewer. The user explicitly requested this model configuration for this verification pass.

## Results

- Live new answers and citation mapping, contextual follow-up, quitting during generation without losing an existing saved entry, and Ctrl-C recovery passed.
- Live CLI restart failed three times. Chrome restart was not attempted because same-conversation restoration was already blocked.
- A controlled check against the real Google sign-in page displayed the attention message. The natural signed-out conversation flow produced a fresh/wrong-conversation state rather than a sign-in redirect, so end-to-end sign-in handling remains unproven. See the [live report](M0-live-2026-09-21.md).
- The final automated suite passed **18/18**, with no failures or skipped tests. The independent reviewer also ran the eight new focused checks successfully.

## Safety fixes

The verification found and fixed three independent gaps: a prompt could go to a different conversation after sign-in or browser navigation; the periodic save could overwrite the saved conversation with that different conversation; and a restored unfinished answer could accept another prompt too early. The fixes validate identity before showing a recovered answer or sending a follow-up, preserve the saved conversation, and keep unfinished answers in recovery. Successful sign-in recovery also updates the recent-session selection and permits same-conversation URL refreshes.

These fixes retain strict conversation validation. They do not solve the live restoration failure.
